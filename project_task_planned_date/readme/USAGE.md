- Open a task and fill in *Planned Dates*. With an allocated time and only a
  start, the end is computed on the company's working hours (e.g. a start on
  Monday at 14:00 with 4 hours on an 8:00–16:00 calendar ends on Tuesday at
  10:00).
- In the task list, show the *Planned Start* and *Planned End* columns from the
  optional columns menu.
- In the task search, filter on *Planned* or *Not Planned* tasks, on a period of
  the planned start, or group by planned start.
